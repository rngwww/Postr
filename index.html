POSTR GUIDE

GUIDE 1 / 5

Swipe Gestures

Swipe a card to the right until the orange underlay expands to edit contents or alarms.
Swipe to the left until the red underlay expands to move it to the 24-hour trash bin.

GUIDE 2 / 5

24-Hour Retention

Deleted reminders stay in the Recently Deleted modal for exactly 24 hours with live countdown indicators. You can restore them or permanently delete them.

GUIDE 3 / 5

Checklists & Menus

Switch to Checklist mode to track actionable tasks with live progress bars. Tap the three dots icon on any card to access native options (Duplicate, Pin, Snooze, Complete All).

GUIDE 4 / 5

Pinning & Drag Reorder

Tap the pin icon to keep high-priority notes at the top. Press and hold any card for a moment to drag and drop it into a customized sort order.

GUIDE 5 / 5

Due Dates & Alarms

Assign scheduled due times to receive lock screen notifications. Configure recurring alarm intervals to keep persistent reminders sticky.
