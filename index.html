POSTR GUIDE
SLIDE 1 / 4

TRIPLE TAP SHORTCUT SETUP

1. Open Apple's Shortcuts app on iPhone.
2. Create a new shortcut, tap Add Action, search for Open URLs.
3. Paste https://rngwww.github.io/Postr/ and name it "Postr Quick Pin".

SLIDE 2 / 4

BIND TO BACK TAP

1. Open iOS Settings > Accessibility > Touch.
2. Scroll to the bottom and select Back Tap.
3. Choose Triple Tap, scroll down to the Shortcuts category, and select Postr Quick Pin.

SLIDE 3 / 4

GESTURES & CONTROLS

Press & Hold: Hold any note to bring up the edit sheet.
Swipe Left: Slide the card left with your finger. Releasing on the left deletes it.

SLIDE 4 / 4

PERSISTENT DRAWER

Tap the bell icon at the top right to enable lock screen notifications so pings sound even when your screen is locked.
