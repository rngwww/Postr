POSTR GUIDE
SLIDE 1 / 6

SWIPE ACTIONS

Swipe Right (Orange): Pull card right until notebook icon expands to edit contents, category, or due times.
Swipe Left (Red): Pull card left until trash icon expands to move note to the 24-hour Trash Bin.

SLIDE 2 / 6

24-HOUR TRASH & RESTORE

Deleted notes are stored in the Trash Bin for 24 hours before permanent removal.
Tap the Trash icon in the header to view countdown timers, restore notes back to your feed, or empty the bin.

SLIDE 3 / 6

CHECKLISTS & IOS MENUS

Checklists: Switch from [ Note ] to [ Checklist ] in the creator to build interactive to-do lists with live progress bars.
iOS Menus: Tap ••• on any card or checklist to access native action menus (Duplicate, Pin, Trash, Complete All).

SLIDE 4 / 6

PIN TO TOP & CATEGORIES

Pin Note: Tap the 📌 icon to stick critical notes to the very top of your feed.
Categories: Tap a category pill to filter. Tap the active category again to unselect it and view all notes.

SLIDE 5 / 6

DUE DATES & ALARM

Specific Due Time: Set an exact alarm time to receive lock screen notifications when due.
Alarm: Choose 1m, 5m, 1h, or custom intervals to keep sticky reminders active.

SLIDE 6 / 6

DATA BACKUP & BACK TAP

Backup & Restore: Tap the POSTR logo to export or import complete JSON backups.
Triple Tap Pin: Set up an Apple Shortcut to Open URLs with Postr's link, and bind it to Back Tap in iPhone Accessibility settings.
